## Usage notes

- Instagram: choose a branded tag, 2–4 local tags, and 4–8 topic tags.
- VK: prioritize Russian local and community tags, with a few English branded tags.
- Telegram and Threads: use fewer tags, usually 0–4, only when they help discovery.
- Rotate sets according to the post: meetup announcement, question prompt, member story, or local community post.
