## Russian-law research

- [152-FZ, Ministry of Labour consolidated publication](https://mintrud.gov.ru/docs/laws/130): Articles 5/6 require a purpose and an appropriate basis; Article 9 addresses consent; Articles 18/18.1, 19, 21 and 22 concern collection, transparency/security, responses and notification. This source includes the 2025 amendments but is not assumed to capture every later amendment.
- [23-FZ official publication, 28 February 2025](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202502280034) and [current Article 18(5)](https://www.consultant.ru/document/Cons_doc_LAW_61801/cbf4e15b7c330f9372e876cdf2bc928bad7950ef/): localization restrictions effective 1 July 2025 require a serious review of collecting Russian citizens’ data using a foreign database. Do not describe the Paris project as automatically compliant, or assume consent cures a localization problem.
- [156-FZ official publication, 24 June 2025](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202506240021) and [Article 5 of that amendment](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_508287/1500545af6526db1dfa11aba44d4df42208a0b05/): from 1 September 2025 consent must be separate from other documents/information confirmed or signed. This matters if consent is the chosen lawful basis.
- [Current Article 12](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/e4ebbe1780de623c7cf32a59ca82a7bb523a25dd/) and [Article 22](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/d996966e22e1320c9de1ab82d9f6be12c3d9d765/) distinguish cross-border-transfer notice from the general operator notice. The owner must verify applicability, recipients/countries, filings and any restrictions. A foreign provider contract does not by itself answer those questions.

Direct retrieval of the official publication pages and regulator portal timed out in this environment; their publication identifiers were located, and the accessible Ministry/consolidated texts were cross-checked. Have Russian counsel verify the current provisions and factual applicability before release. No regulator filing has been made by this work.

A region setting is not a complete processor/transfer inventory.

[Pre-clearance documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/additional-configuration/hostname-management/pre-clearance/) confirms that a clearance cookie depends on configuration.
- Source inspection found no app-written first-party cookies, advertising pixels, Google Analytics or Yandex Metrica. Local/session storage are inventoried separately. A mandatory security widget is not described as a marketing tracker. No sham consent banner has been added: confirm production behavior and legal bases first; add actual blocking/preferences if a non-essential technology later requires consent.
