## Russian discovery

The English-first site includes short, human Russian explanations on the About and Contact pages for users searching for a conversation club in Сергиев Посад. A complete Russian version should only be added when it can be maintained as a genuinely useful translation with its own canonical URL and `hreflang` relationship.

Natural Russian themes to monitor in Search Console:

- разговорный клуб английского языка Сергиев Посад

## Guardrails

- Do not add meta keywords.
- Do not repeat exact-match phrases in every heading or internal link.
- Do not publish an Event result until a real meetup has a confirmed date, venue, price, and URL.
- Keep the exact venue private until the club has a confirmed public announcement.
